This second small project was fully focused on utilising movement features in JavaScript. By using the let i function, I was able to create a simple image that shows the day and night cycle. Utilising some of my knowledge from the previous drawing project, I created a background of a blue sky and green grass, and then added movement to a yellow circle, symbolising the sun, and a grey circle, symbolising the moon. I have both of these circles move across the image to bring across the idea of the sun and moon creating a day and night cycle. I also coded the movement to reset once both circles reach a certain point, to keep the circles in a movement cycle.
